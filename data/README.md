Dataset – Bank Marketing Analysis

This project uses the Bank Marketing Dataset to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

The dataset contains information about bank clients, their financial details, campaign contacts, and whether they subscribed to a term deposit.

Dataset Information:
- Dataset Name: Bank Marketing Dataset
- Total Records: 45,211
- Total Features: 17
- Target Variable: "y"
- Target Categories: Yes / No

Dataset Source:
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing

Purpose of Analysis:
The dataset is used to understand customer behaviour and identify patterns associated with term deposit subscriptions.

Main Analysis Areas:
- Customer demographics
- Job and education categories
- Marital status
- Account balance
- Housing and personal loans
- Campaign contacts
- Previous campaign outcomes
- Call duration
- Subscription behaviour
